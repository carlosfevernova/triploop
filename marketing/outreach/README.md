5 personalized outreach messages for Tier 1 targets. Copy-paste ready, one per file.

## Follow-up cadence per file

Each file has "Cadence" section documenting when to follow up. Default:
- Day 0: initial message
- Day 5-7: follow-up (varies per target)
- Day 14-21: third touch (only if warm)
- Day 30: stop and refocus

## After 30 days

If none of Tier 1 converts to a serious eval:
- Move to **Tier 2** (Tiny, SureSwift, GuideGeek — see LISTINGS.md for full list)
- Consider price adjustment down to $28-30K firm
- Explore **BUNDLE** with FiestaMap at $40K (see BUNDLE.md)
- Post publicly on r/SideProject weekly for-sale thread + Hacker News Show HN

## What NOT to do

- **Don't personalize beyond the templates** — the templates are already personalized. Over-personalizing signals desperation.
- **Don't send more than 5 first-touches per week** — creates spammy pattern that hurts reputation.
- **Don't reduce price in the first message** — hold $35K firm through Day 30. Discount only after two serious eval-then-no responses.
- **Don't reply to "not interested" with more pitch** — thank them, ask if they know someone who'd fit, move on.
